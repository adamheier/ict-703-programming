#### **Assessment Task 3 – Certiport Exam This Week**

A friendly reminder that Assessment Task 3 will take place during your usual scheduled workshop in Week 12. Please make sure you carefully review the Assessment Task 3 instructions on Canvas and complete the necessary preparations before attending your exam.

Before the Exam

1. Register for Certiport: Make sure you have registered using your student email address and your name exactly as printed on your student ID. After registering, you must add your student ID number to your Certiport profile. Please check that your name and date of birth are correct, as these details cannot easily be changed after registration.
    
2. Remember your Certiport login credentials: You will need to enter your username and password during the exam. Please make sure you know these details, as saved passwords in your regular browser or password manager may not be accessible within the Certiport browser.
    
3. Prepare your photo ID: Your student ID card is recommended, but a passport, driver's licence, or another acceptable form of photo ID can also be used.
    

Please follow your proctor's instructions throughout the session and ask for assistance if you experience any technical difficulties.
