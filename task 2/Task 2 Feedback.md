**This is an excellent submission that demonstrates very good software engineering practices, strong analytical thinking and a high level of attention to program design and testing.**

### Strengths

- The strongest aspect of this submission is the overall architecture of the solution. The separation between validation, storage, fraud detection, account analysis and reporting creates a clear and maintainable design that is easy to follow and extend. Your discussion of why these design decisions were made demonstrates the level of analysis expected at postgraduate level rather than simply documenting what was built.
- The advanced functionality is particularly impressive. Enhanced validation, configurable fraud rules, customer account analysis, time-based fraud detection and reporting features all provide meaningful extensions beyond the core requirements. The testing strategy is also excellent, with comprehensive coverage across core functionality, advanced features, boundary conditions and full-system behaviour. This provides strong evidence that the solution has been thoroughly validated.

### Areas for Improvement

- While the report already demonstrates a strong level of analysis, future work could further strengthen the discussion of alternative design approaches and architectural trade-offs. Comparing multiple possible solutions and justifying the final choice would elevate the evaluation even further.
- The customer account analysis and configurable rule framework are very good extensions. Future projects could continue this progression through behavioural analysis, risk scoring, investigation workflows or database-backed case management features.
- Although appropriately acknowledged in the report, the use of floating-point values for monetary calculations would ideally be replaced with Decimal in a production-quality financial application.
- Future testing could include performance and scalability evaluation to demonstrate how the solution behaves under significantly larger transaction volumes.
